Event Verification Workflow

1
Student Registers Student registers for your event as a participant or student coordinator.
2
Dynamic QR Generate a short-lived QR token while the event is active.
3
Face Verification Student completes live camera-based face verification.
4
Teacher Notification Relevant teachers receive verification notifications for their students.

Important

Event verification and academic lecture attendance are separate systems. A successful event verification confirms the student's presence at the event, but it does not automatically mark the student's academic lecture attendance.
