System Workflow

How EventSync handles event verification.

1
Registration

Student registers for the selected event as a participant or student coordinator.

2
Dynamic QR

Event coordinator generates a short-lived QR token which is validated on the server.

3
Face Verification

The student's captured face is compared against the registered face image.

4
Teacher Notification

Assigned teachers/coordinators receive a notification showing verified student details.

5
Manual Attendance

Academic lecture attendance is still manually marked by the teacher.
